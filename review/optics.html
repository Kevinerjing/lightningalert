Convex Lens: Candle and Screen Lab

Drag the candle and the cardboard screen. The screen shows a sharp image only when it is placed at the correct image distance.
Drag the candle on the left.
Drag the cardboard screen on the right.
Experiment rule:
Move the screen until the projected image becomes sharp. When the candle is at 2F, the sharp image appears at 2F and is the same size.

This simulation uses the thin-lens equation. The screen image becomes blurrier as the screen moves away from the correct image position.
