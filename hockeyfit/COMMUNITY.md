# HockeyFit community pilot

HockeyFit is intended to stay free for players, families, coaches, and minor hockey associations.

## Pilot goal

Help first-year hockey families arrive at an equipment shop with a sensible starting point for stick length and flex, while teaching them what to verify before cutting or buying.

## Suggested pilot sequence

1. Ask two experienced coaches or equipment volunteers to review the questions and results.
2. Run a small test with 10–20 Kanata families across several age groups.
3. Record only anonymous outcome counts: recommendations completed, in-store checks completed, and whether the suggested fit needed adjustment.
4. Publish a short revision log explaining what Eric learned and changed.
5. Offer the reviewed version to a local minor hockey association, then expand to other associations.

## Evidence of impact

Useful evidence includes families helped, participating associations, coach feedback, result accuracy after an in-store check, accessibility improvements, and major product decisions. Do not collect children's names, photos, or contact information without a clear consent and privacy process.

## Photo Fit beta

The one-photo flow opens the device camera with an on-screen standing silhouette, floor line, stick guide, and chin-to-nose fit zone. After capture, the player or parent taps only the top of the stick. The result checks standing length; it does not infer flex or blade lie from a static photo. The frame is held temporarily in browser memory, cleared after the result or when the camera closes, and is never uploaded or permanently stored. Any future server-side or automated vision version requires explicit parental consent, deletion rules, storage rules, model limitations, and a non-identifying test dataset.
