🚀 How to Play

Mission: Reach Sol 300 with a working habitat and at least one potato harvest.

  1. Plant 10 seeds before pressing Start.
  2. Press Start and choose a speed. Pause whenever you need to plan.
  3. Balance irrigation, grow lights, heating and recycling. Watch food, water, battery and habitat health.
  4. When growth reaches 70%, you may harvest early for fewer potatoes. Waiting until 100% gives the best yield. Click Harvest, then plant the next crop.
  5. Respond to dust storms, leaks and equipment failures. Emergencies pause the clock.

Win: Survive to Sol 300 after at least one harvest. Lose: Food or habitat health reaches zero, or rescue arrives without any harvest.

Tip: Food is consumed at 1,540 kcal per sol. The dashboard shows how many sols of food remain. The starting supplies and yields are game-balanced estimates, not values from the novel.

Science Mode (optional)

Potato calories

Food energy ≈ edible potato mass (g) × 0.77 kcal/g. A potato-only diet cannot provide every essential nutrient.

Electricity

Energy (kWh) = Power (kW) × Time (hours). Heating, lighting and recycling consume energy.

Photosynthesis

Plants use light energy, carbon dioxide and water to produce sugars. Limited light can slow growth.

Martian time

One sol ≈ 24 hours 39 minutes 35 seconds, or 1.02749 Earth days.

Simulation accuracy

Resource rates, crop yields and emergencies are fictional, balanced gameplay values, not validated Mars agriculture predictions.
