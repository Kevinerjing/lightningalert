Varsity Tennis

From singles to doubles. Learning how to make a team stronger.

01 / Commitment

Two seasons. New challenges.

I train in tennis twice a week and earned a spot on my school's varsity tennis team in both Grade 9 and Grade 10. In Grade 9, I played singles, where I needed to make decisions and adjust strategy on my own. In Grade 10, my coach assigned me to doubles, which challenged me in a different way.

02 / Practice

The work behind the match.

My doubles partner and I had different strengths: I was more comfortable serving, while he was stronger at volleys near the net. We arranged additional practice sessions outside regular team training to work on positioning, serve coordination and playing to each other's strengths.

After practice, we talked about what worked and what we could improve. Those conversations helped us understand how to play together instead of just playing beside each other.

Positioning & volleys

03 / Teamwork

Focus on the next ball.

When my partner made several mistakes in a row, I tried to encourage him not to show frustration. I knew it could increase our opponents' confidence and put more pressure on us. Instead, I reminded him to focus on the next ball.

We advanced to the regional finals. I was proud of the result, but even more proud of how we learned to support each other.

“A good teammate helps you move forward after mistakes.”
