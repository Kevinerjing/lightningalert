Gemistry Studio

Team member · 1.5 years, starting in Grade 8 · 4 jewelry-making workshops

At Gemistry Studio, I helped prepare and run jewelry-making workshops while independently managing the team's YouTube channel. The experience brought together hands-on creativity, public speaking and programming.

Workshop preparation and support

Our team took turns preparing bead packets, setting up the space, making samples and preparing presentation content. I introduced activities and helped participants who needed extra support complete their jewelry and take it home. I learned to pay attention to people throughout an activity, even after the introduction was over.

YouTube channel

I independently handled filming, editing, voiceovers, thumbnails, titles and uploads. Taking a video from filming to publication helped me improve my product photography and editing. Preparing samples and helping participants also strengthened my jewelry design skills.

YouTube statistics script

Our team originally tracked YouTube statistics by hand. I wrote a Google Apps Script to collect video information, views, likes and comments into Google Sheets for internal use, helping the team follow its promotional goals. It showed me how programming could improve a real process beyond a school assignment.
