01 / Science & Leadership

Sharing a passion for weather.

I started the Weather & Climate Club in Grade 9 because our school had no club focused on weather or climate. I prepared discussions and demonstrations about weather stations, lightning detection and air quality monitoring.

02 / Sports & Teamwork

From singles to doubles.

After playing singles in Grade 9, I moved to doubles in Grade 10. My partner and I arranged extra practices to work on our positioning, serves and net play. We eventually reached the regional finals.

03 / Music

Finding my place in an ensemble.

I play saxophone in my school band. Rehearsing together has taught me to listen closely to other sections and keep improving my part. Our band achieved the Gold standard at Capital Region Musicfest and earned an invitation to Nationals.

04 / Technology & Creativity

Turning questions into projects.

I created Element Hero, an online chemistry card game inspired by my Grade 6 platinum superhero project. The cards use real properties of elements and compounds, and I shared the game with classmates and teachers.

Explore Element Hero ↗
05 / Weather Observation & Volunteering

Observing weather beyond the classroom.

In February 2025, I joined CoCoRaHS as a volunteer. The Community Collaborative Rain, Hail and Snow Network brings together volunteers who observe and report precipitation in their communities.

It gave me another way to connect my interest in meteorology with real-world weather observation.
